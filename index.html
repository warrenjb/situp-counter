How it works

The sensor measures the angle of your torso compared with lying flat. Each time you start a set, it takes a fresh "flat" reading, so it doesn't matter exactly how it sits on the strap.

A rep counts when you rise past the up line and come back below the down line. Reaching the partial line but not the top counts as a half-rep.

Counting happens on the sensor itself, so no reps are lost if Bluetooth drops for a moment.

Doing a workout

  1. Strap it onSensor flat on the middle of your chest, snug enough not to bounce.
  2. ConnectOpen the Counter in Chrome, tap Connect, and pick "SitupCounter". Sign in once so your sets are saved.
  3. Lie down and tap Start setHold still for a second while it calibrates, then go when you hear "Go".
  4. Tap Stop set when you're doneThe set is saved automatically and shows up on the Dashboard.

First time? Open "Tune rep detection" on the Counter and run Auto-tune to fit the thresholds to your range of motion.
